Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Next Day Movers uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites work and remember preferences.

What we use

Our website itself sets no advertising or analytics tracking cookies. We only rely on the strictly necessary functionality your browser provides to display the site.

Third-party services

  • Google Maps — the coverage map on our Contact page is embedded from Google and may set its own cookies when it loads.
  • FormSubmit — processes your quote or contact form submission; it does not set cookies on our site.

If we introduce analytics or marketing tools in future, we will update this policy and add a cookie-consent banner where required.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking third-party cookies may affect the embedded map but will not stop the rest of the site working.
