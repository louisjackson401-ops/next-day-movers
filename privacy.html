Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Next Day Movers ("we", "us") collects and uses your personal information when you use this website or request a quote. You can contact us at nextdaymoversuk@gmail.com or 07777 622437.

What we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us through our quote and contact forms: your name, phone number or email, the postcodes and dates for your move, and any message you send. We do not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use your details solely to prepare your estimate, respond to your enquiry and arrange your move. The lawful basis is to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

Who we share it with

We never sell your data. We use a small number of trusted service providers to run the site:

  • FormSubmit — delivers your form submission to our email inbox.
  • postcodes.io — only the postcodes you enter are sent, to estimate the distance for your quote.
  • Google Maps — the coverage map embedded on our Contact page is served by Google, which may set its own cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting move, and to meet our legal obligations.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to our use of your data, and to data portability. To exercise any of these, email nextdaymoversuk@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the date above shows when it was last revised.
