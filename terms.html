Last updated: 18 July 2026

These terms apply to quotes and removal, man-and-van and waste-clearance services provided by Next Day Movers. Please read them alongside any written quote we send you.

Quotes & estimates

The instant estimate shown on this website is a guide only and is not a binding quote. A fixed price is confirmed by our team after we have reviewed the details of your move. Prices can vary with access, volume, distance and timing.

Booking

All bookings are subject to availability. We may ask for a deposit to secure your moving date, which is deducted from the final balance.

Your responsibilities

Please give us accurate information about your move, ensure suitable parking and access at both addresses, and tell us about valuable, fragile or bulky items in advance.

Items we cannot move

For safety and legal reasons we cannot transport hazardous materials (flammable liquids, gas, paints), perishable food, certain plants or pets, or illegal items.

Insurance & liability

We carry goods-in-transit and public liability insurance, and we are a registered waste carrier for clearance work. Any claim should be reported to us promptly. Our liability is limited to the terms of our cover; we are not liable for pre-existing damage or for items packed by the customer that we did not pack.

Cancellation & rescheduling

If you need to cancel or move your date, please give us as much notice as possible. Deposits for cancellations at short notice may be non-refundable to cover held capacity.
